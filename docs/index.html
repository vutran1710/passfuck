Enter your name, email and phrase twice. The phrase stays hidden as you type.

Make it 6 or more invented words, not a quote. Write it on paper.

What's public

Anyone can see

  • Your aliases
  • When entries changed

Only you can read

  • Sites, usernames and passwords
  • Notes and one-time codes

Anyone can download the vault and guess at your phrase, so make it long and invented.
